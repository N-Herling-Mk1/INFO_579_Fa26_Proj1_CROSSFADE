What stays the same across all three projects: the business and the data it has to manage.

The business

CROSSFADE is a streaming service for independent artists. Artists upload recordings directly under their own licence, so the catalogue is almost entirely new material. Every track is scored at upload by several versions of the BEARDOWN genre classifier, each returning a probability for all ten genres. Listeners don't browse genre names. They build stations from a target blend (say 60% jazz, 40% hip hop) and an allowed range of ambiguity, so a track uploaded an hour ago is as findable as one with a million plays.

The database holds the artist roster and catalogue, the model versions and every result they produced, listener accounts and their stations, and every play, which is both the taste signal and the billable event for royalties.
